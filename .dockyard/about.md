Define your data models once and generate idiomatic Swift and Kotlin from them, so your
iOS and Android apps share one source of truth for their model layer.

Write models in a small, readable DSL and watch the generated code for both platforms
update beside it as you type. ModelForge keeps the two languages honest with each other:
integer widths are explicit, defaults mean the same thing on both sides, and generation is
deterministic, so regenerating only touches the files that actually changed.
